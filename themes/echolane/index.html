FAQ

Asked often enough to answer here

Can I import the feedback I already have?

Yes. Upload a CSV or connect your inbox and Echolane imports up to 12 months of history on the first sync.

How accurate is the automatic grouping?

Most teams keep about nine out of ten suggested themes as they are. Anything else you can merge or split in seconds.

Is our customers' data used to train models?

No. Your data is only used to group your own feedback and is never shared with other workspaces.

We are two people. Is this too much tool?

The Solo plan is free and built exactly for that stage: two sources, automatic themes and nothing to configure.
