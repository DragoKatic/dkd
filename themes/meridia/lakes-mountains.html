Description

Easy to moderate walks between three lakes, with boats doing the long stretches and nights in a lakeside hotel and a mountain hut.

Luggage is moved for you every day; you only carry water and a camera.

Itinerary

  1. Days 1–2

    The first lake

    Boat crossing and a walk along the shore.

  2. Days 3–5

    Into the mountains

    Hut-to-hut walking with lake views from above.

  3. Days 6–7

    The last lake

    A swim, a picnic and a farewell dinner.

What's included

  • 6 nights in hotels and a mountain hut
  • Half board
  • Walking guide and luggage transfers
  • All boat crossings

Not included: flights to the start of the tour, lunches unless listed, personal expenses.
