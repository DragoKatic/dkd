About us

A small agency of eleven people who would rather show you one valley properly than ten cities in a hurry.

Our story

It started with a missed train

In 2009 our founder missed the last train out of a mountain village and spent three unplanned days there. Those were the best days of the trip — so we began planning journeys that leave room for exactly that.

Today we run eleven regular tours, all bookable online, and around two hundred tailor-made trips a year, still planned by the same small team.
