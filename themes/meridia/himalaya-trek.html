Description

A moderate trek through the foothills, five to six hours of walking a day, with porters carrying your bag between family tea houses.

Rest days are built in, so there is time for monasteries, villages and the view at sunrise.

Itinerary

  1. Days 1–3

    Arrival and the first villages

    City tour, drive to the trailhead and the first tea house.

  2. Days 4–10

    On the trail

    Ridges, rhododendron forests and a sunrise viewpoint.

  3. Days 11–14

    Down to the lake

    Back to the valley and two easy days by the lake.

What's included

  • 13 nights in hotels and tea houses
  • All meals on the trek
  • Mountain guide and porters
  • Trekking permits

Not included: flights to the start of the tour, lunches unless listed, personal expenses.
