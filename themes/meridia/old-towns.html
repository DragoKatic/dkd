Description

Three capitals on the Danube and the Vltava, linked by comfortable daytime trains. You stay in small family-run hotels in the old towns, so every morning starts with a walk instead of a transfer.

Each city has a local guide for a half-day walk and a free afternoon with our list of cafés, markets and viewpoints.

Itinerary

  1. Days 1–3

    Prague

    Old Town walk, the castle at sunrise and a beer-hall dinner.

  2. Days 4–5

    Vienna

    Train to Vienna, coffee-house tour and an evening concert.

  3. Days 6–8

    Budapest

    Danube views, thermal baths and a farewell dinner by the river.

What's included

  • 7 nights in central boutique hotels with breakfast
  • 2nd-class train tickets between the cities
  • Local guide in every city
  • 3 dinners and a concert ticket

Not included: flights to the start of the tour, lunches unless listed, personal expenses.
