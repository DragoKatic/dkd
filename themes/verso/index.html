The story

Liora grew up between the Adriatic coast and Belgrade and was scouted at nineteen on a night train to Vienna. Six years later she walks for ateliers in Milan and Paris and is the face of quiet luxury campaigns from Lisbon to Dubrovnik.

She is known for her stillness: long, unhurried takes, a sense for light and the patience to wait for the one frame that tells the whole story. Off set she studies art history and collects old fashion magazines.

She doesn't pose for the camera. She waits, and lets the light find her.

RM R. Marchetti, creative director, Maison Lune
