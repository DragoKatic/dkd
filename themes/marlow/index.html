About me

Hi, I'm Iva

I grew up drawing on the back of my parents' shop receipts and never stopped. After studying graphic design I worked for six years in a branding studio before going freelance in 2019.

Today I work from a small studio by the river with too many plants and one very opinionated cat. I love briefs with a clear story, bright colours and a little humour.
