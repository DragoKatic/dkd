Our tribe

Born at a stall in the forest

We started in 2015 selling hand-printed hoodies from a tent at a small forest festival. Today twelve makers print, sew and test everything we sell, and we still pack every order ourselves.

Made to dance in

Everything is tested on real dance floors before it goes into the shop: through dusty days, cold nights and at least one sunrise set.

We print with water-based and UV-reactive inks, sew from organic and recycled fabrics and keep every run small, so nothing ends up in landfill.
