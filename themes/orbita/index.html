// faq

Questions, answered

Can't find what you are looking for? Our team replies within one business hour.

Is my money safe with Orbita?

Customer funds are held in segregated accounts at our licensed e-money partner and are never used for lending.

How long does a payout take?

SEPA Instant payouts arrive in seconds. Other local transfers usually arrive the same business day.

Which businesses can open an account?

Registered companies and sole traders from the EU, the UK and Switzerland. More countries are coming soon.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. You can switch plans or cancel at any time from the settings page; changes apply from the next billing period.
