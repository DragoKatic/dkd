Create an account

A registration form that explains itself: a live password strength meter, a matching check and a clear consent box.

At least 8 characters. Mix letters, numbers and a symbol.

Why this create account form works

  • The strength meter tells people what to add, not only that the password is weak.
  • First and last name sit side by side on wide screens and stack on phones.
  • Consent is a separate, unticked checkbox, as GDPR expects.
  • Connects to WordPress user registration or a WooCommerce customer account.
