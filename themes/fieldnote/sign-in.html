Why this sign-in form works

  • Labels stay visible above the fields, so people never forget what to type.
  • The show-password button saves a second try on mobile keyboards.
  • Errors appear next to the field and in a summary, and are read out by screen readers.
  • In WordPress it can replace the default wp-login.php screen or sit in a members page.
