Why this multi-step project inquiry works

  • Three short steps feel lighter than one long form, so more people finish.
  • Each step is checked before moving on, and Back keeps what was typed.
  • Without JavaScript all steps show as one form, so nobody is locked out.
  • Ideal for agencies, builders and consultants running WordPress sites.
