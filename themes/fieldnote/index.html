What every form does

Small details that make people finish

  • Labels that stayEvery field has a visible label, never a placeholder that disappears while typing.
  • Errors that helpMessages say what to fix, sit next to the field and are announced to screen readers.
  • Built for thumbsLarge touch targets, the right mobile keyboard for each field and no sideways scrolling.
  • Works without JavaScriptMulti-step forms fall back to one page, so no visitor is ever locked out.

How the forms fit into your WordPress site

The designs are plain, accessible HTML and CSS, so they can be built into a custom WordPress theme or used to style the form plugin you already have.

  1. Pick the formsChoose the forms your site needs and the fields for each of them.
  2. Match your brandColours, fonts and wording follow your theme, so the forms feel native.
  3. Connect and testLogin and registration use WordPress users, the checkout uses WooCommerce, and every form is tested on phones.
