Video

Two short, muted video clips are used in the demo websites. The Oakmoss barber shop clip is Pexels video 3998516 by cottonbro studio, free under the Pexels licence; it is slowed to half speed, scaled to 1024 px wide and compressed. The Brightcut showreel is a six-second excerpt of “Camera Operators, Television, Video, & Film at My Next Move” by CareerOneStop (U.S. Department of Labor), source on Wikimedia Commons, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; it is shortened to one shot, scaled to 960 px wide, muted and compressed, and stays under the same licence.
